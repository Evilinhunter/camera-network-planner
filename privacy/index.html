Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

The short version: this site doesn't collect personal data. There are no accounts, no forms that send data anywhere, no analytics and no advertising trackers.

Your job details stay in your browser

Everything you type into the tools is processed in your browser. Your inputs are saved in your browser's local storage so it's there when you come back. It isn't sent to me or to anyone else. You can clear it by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Services the site relies on

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub may log technical details of visits, such as IP addresses, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. Your browser requests them from Google, which receives your IP address. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

Cookies

This site doesn't set cookies. If you follow a link to a store such as Amazon or B&H, that store may set its own cookies, including ones that record the visit came from this site. Those are covered by the store's own privacy policy.

Email

If you email me, I'll use your address only to reply, and won't share it.

Changes

If the site ever adds analytics or anything else that collects data, this page will be updated first and will say what is collected and why.
