About Camera Network Planner

Camera Network Planner is a free tool for security camera, access control and AV installers. You list the devices on a job, and it gives you the handoff the customer's IT team asks for: the IP scheme, firewall requests and port map, with the PoE budget checked and switch setup written for Cisco, Aruba, Arista and MikroTik.

Who it's for

Integrators and installers who put cameras, door controllers and recorders on networks but aren't network engineers, and the IT teams who have to approve that work. It covers jobs on the customer's network in VLANs, on a separate security network, and with cameras plugged straight into a PoE recorder.

Who made it

It's an independent side project by a working network engineer, built on my own time. It isn't affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any manufacturer, distributor or employer.

The source is public on GitHub under the MIT license.

How it's paid for

The tool is free. Some gear links may be affiliate links, which can earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. The affiliate disclosure explains how that works.
