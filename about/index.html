About Installer Network Tools

Installer Network Tools is a set of free tools for security camera, access control and AV installers:

  • Camera network planner: list the devices on a job and get the handoff the customer's IT team asks for, with the IP scheme, firewall requests and port map, the PoE budget checked, and switch setup for Cisco, Aruba, Arista and MikroTik.
  • NVR storage calculator: storage, bandwidth and drive sizes from your cameras and retention.
  • PoE budget calculator: total draw against a switch's budget, with a per-port check.
  • Subnet and VLAN planner: an IP plan for sites and VLANs, with a CSV export.

Who it's for

Integrators and installers who put cameras, door controllers and recorders on networks but aren't network engineers, and the IT teams who have to approve that work. The camera planner covers jobs on the customer's network in VLANs, on a separate security network, and with cameras plugged straight into a PoE recorder.

Who made it
